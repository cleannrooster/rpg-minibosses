## Sound effects

`common/src/main/resources/assets/rpg-minibosses/sounds/boss/**/*.ogg` are converted (mono,
silence-trimmed except loops, Ogg Vorbis) from two OpenGameArt packs by `tools/convert_sounds.py`,
which also lists which source file backs which sound. The converted files remain under their source
licences; the rest of the mod keeps its own licence.

  https://opengameart.org/content/jc-sounds-fantasy-sfx-pack-vol-1 -
  licensed CC-BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: converted from
  WAV to mono Ogg Vorbis, leading and trailing silence trimmed (loops left untrimmed).
- **Spell Sounds Starter Pack** -
  https://opengameart.org/content/spell-sounds-starter-pack -
  offered under CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0 or GPL 2.0; used here under CC-BY-SA 3.0
